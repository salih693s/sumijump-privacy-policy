Privacy Policy – Sumi Jump

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sumi Jump collects, uses and protects information when you play the game.

2. Information We Collect

The App and its service providers may process:

  • Player nicknames and game scores submitted to the online leaderboard.
  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • IP addresses and approximate location.
  • Advertising interaction and usage information.
  • Technical and diagnostic information.

3. Advertising

Sumi Jump uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location and advertising interaction data.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Online Leaderboard

Players can submit nicknames and scores to an online leaderboard powered by Supabase. Nicknames and scores may be visible to other players. Please do not use personal information as your nickname.

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

5. How We Use Information

  • To operate the game and online leaderboard.
  • To display and measure advertisements.
  • To maintain and improve app performance.
  • To identify and resolve technical issues.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that support the App, including Google AdMob and Supabase.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly request children's personal contact information. Please contact us if you believe information has been collected improperly.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.
